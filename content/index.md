## Beliefs

* Growth is as much about how to unlearn what used to work as it is about learning new skills
  * Now is a time to rethink what we think we know about software engineering
  * Psychological blockers we have are things which were adaptive at one point
* As long as coding models progress, the best teams will constantly adapt and rethink their core processes
  * The most effective ICs review work like engineering directors
* The best teams have a fun "core game loop" that leads to continual improvement
  * Applies to health, fitness, and product development
* Judge performance on outcomes but apply learnings to process
  * Life is noisy: good decisions can lose and bad ones can win
  * The process is consistent enough to improve
* Pay attention to the left side of the technology adoption curve
  * It takes years before a trend reaches mainstream acceptance
  * What are we still doing today that is already known by others to be sub-optimal?
* The most persistent disagreements are about incentives, not information
  * If just information, sharing it ends the argument
  * If incentives, the reasons regenerate as fast as you refute them
* Subtraction is systematically underrated for large companies
  * Removing is harder to take credit for than adding
  * Local performance review optimization leads to emergent cultural biases
